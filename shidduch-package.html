3-month package

Helping Families Through the Shidduch Journey

Personal guidance and support, from start to finish.

Get started

The shidduch journey can feel long, confusing, and lonely. You don’t have to carry it by yourself.

Our 3-month package is built for one purpose only: to help you and your family navigate this stage with clarity, confidence, and calm.

What you’ll receive

  • A private intake conversation to understand your child, your family, and your goals
  • Clear guidance on the process: what to do, what to leave alone, and when
  • Support with resume, references, and communication with shadchanim
  • Practical advice for handling suggestions, research, and decisions
  • Chizuk and encouragement for you as parents along the way
  • Regular check-in calls or messages, e.g. weekly or every two weeks

How it works

1

Reach out.

Send us a short message to get started.

2

First session.

We learn about your family and set priorities.

3

Three months of guidance.

Ongoing support, with a clear plan at each step.

4

Review.

At the end, we look back at the progress and plan next steps.

Who it’s for

Families who want an experienced, caring person beside them as they go through shidduchim.
